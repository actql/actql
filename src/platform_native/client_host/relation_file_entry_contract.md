# Non-Model relation Work entry custody

This contract is limited to the original single-Run, non-Model relation-file
entry. It is not a Model inventory bootstrap, CLI installation, authorization
issuer, child-entry owner or physical-cleanup acknowledgement.

## Original owner and resource split

The original `NativeLocalStoreOperation` continues to own every Read, SQLite
and business scope exchange. Its exact retained execution owns the business
Ledger. The existing entered-only Ledger-borrow API is unchanged.

A new opaque arena entry is prepared while the operation's original Host Read
is entered and its business execution is unentered. It retains the same Host,
Read/pair, execution and exact business capacity token. The Ledger is not
returned from this preparation. The exact prepared-binding cache must have been established by generic acceptance
in this same original operation. Primitive guards check it before preparation
and immediately before each business validator; absent, replaced or invalidated
cache state refuses without reseeding. The original validator still runs on its
cache-hit path. Successful lookup, dispatch and first-start preserve this cache;
rollback, lost commit or operation end invalidate it.

The entry debits only the complete business
Work control quote, without issuing an attempt, holding or transfer allowance.

Its constructor, reservation token, immutable usage replacement, imported
current-check intermediates and returned preparation carriers are allocations
of the original Read. Their separately named constructor inventory is composed
into the complete Read consumer envelope before its one-shot admission. An
already-claimed consumer cannot be enlarged; entry preparation requires the
original pre-admitted envelope to already include this named component.
They must not also be counted as business allocations. Business Work, callback,
Common wrapper, failure and scope-return carriers are covered by the business
control token before the first business-side constructor runs.

The client entry has a private constructor. Its projection shares the exact
nominal arena entry and its once-only claim state, rather than creating another
grant. The arena self-marker rejects a copied arena record and is cleared only
after original cancellation or final cleanup. The client binds the exact StoreOperation,
Host, retained execution, request, prepared program, compiled Run, definition,
Row declaration, requirement and acquisition. Caller-created arena entries,
matching CIDs, a copied or foreign arena original and smaller capacity DATA cannot forge this
binding. The actual token must exactly match the computed business quote.

Selection and limits are trusted Host DATA, not another original or grant. The
entry validates their Source-owned bounds before copying the path arrays in its
original Read, and borrows immutable strings and limit records. Work opening
accepts no independent selection or limits: it uses this exact private snapshot.
A later mutation of the caller's arrays cannot change the funded selection.
The caller preparation inventory is derived from these same limits. The first
preflight mapping construction is distinct from the later Source-owned mapping;
it must not be paid twice or assigned to a reservation that does not yet exist.

## Preparation and refusal

Preparation is called only from the original `with_original_read` callback.
Both accepted and refused preparation results belong to its Read inventory.
A reserve failure occurs there, before business entry or durable physical
start. Pristine-Ledger checks belong only to this closed entry. They reject
unrelated live reservations and spent attempts; they do not clear history or
change any global Source rule.

A successful client entry preallocates a separate immutable unavailable outcome
in that same Read. This carrier has no reference to the legitimate entry, Work,
control token or cleanup owner. Its close is a local no-op, with no ability to
advance the legitimate state or permit a second use. Foreign operation/Host/originals, wrong physical scope,
reentry and reuse return that existing outcome without constructors or mutation
of the first outcome. This initial guard uses nominal comparisons and an
allocation-free observation of the already-owned physical scope. It does not
replace subsequent original current, Account or Run checks.

The new Work current/export and cleanup entries also check the exact arena
original, live token and actual business scope before constructing any dynamic
product. Current/export wrong-scope results are separate preowned inert Errs
from Read preparation; Bool cleanup returns false without changing state.
Normal execution and cleanup observations are distinct. Legacy Values and
Resident access helpers are unchanged.

After that guard, the exact pre-owned business control covers all dynamic
checks and their Result/tuple/failure carriers. The client obtains the original
Ledger only in the actual entered business scope. No new external callback can
intervene between the last scope/identity check and that projection.

## Lifetime and return

The arena entry stores no Work, Source, Common execution, result bridge or
business failure. The client entry similarly stores no child outcome. Work and
its original cleanup-owning failure may retain the entry, but the inert
unavailable refusal does not. The entry retains none of them in return.
There is no entry/Work reference cycle.

The existing Journal first-start remains one-shot. A committed claim followed
by a failed current check, lost reply or failed Read return stays claimed. The
entry records the original cleanup obligation and never makes an attempted
Work call ready again.

Business cleanup closes the result bridge, then Common, then Source. The entry
control remains reserved throughout this cleanup and through any failed or
unknown original scope return. Logical close, a false current predicate and a
returned Bool do not acknowledge physical retirement.

The coordinator first completes the same original scope-return and child-cleanup
duties. Outside every child frame, the client entry calls the original
StoreOperation's actual routing-metadata close. Only after that acknowledgement
does it release its own optional access/operation projection. A separate helper
returns before physical cleanup, so its validation temporaries do not themselves
retain the operation. No legacy primary or cleanup issue is cleared. Escaped
Work, failure, operation or captured Journal aliases remain genuine owners and
can continue to block physical retirement.

The arena entry keeps its exact control debit while the existing retained
execution owner stops its imported frame, relocates cleanup DATA into outer
control, and attempts actual C-context retirement. Escaped closed Work or export
aliases produce actual BUSY. The entry neither returns their capacity nor issues
an acknowledgement from a flag. Only the existing owner's successful physical
retirement observation permits exact-token settlement in outer control, before
the existing report and parent return. The entry records its own successful
settlement so later report/retirement failures can retry the same original path.
An unknown report retains the existing sticky uncertainty.

A never-entered entry is separate: all its controls are Read allocations and no
business allocation has borrowed its token. It may be cancelled only in its
same original Read. This does not permit cancelling an entered or unfinished
borrower.

The coordinator retires the remaining entry and Read-side aliases before Read,
pair, domain and arena retirement. These APIs do not restore spent attempts,
transfer or history and do not increase an envelope. Legitimate settlement
returns only the capacity held by the exact original byte reservation.

## Constructor tariff and scope

The pinned Native lowering allocates successful `Ok(Unit)` products. The fixed
Read and business constructor vectors count their concrete call multiplicity;
a zero-payload success is not assumed free. Zero-capture function values and
small view carriers are conservatively included without relying on optimizer
lifting. These are logical representation tariffs, not native page or RSS
measurements.

The ordinary retained-execution close body remains unchanged. Only this new
entry uses a private copy of its physical-close prefix, locked to that original
body by the source-parity regression. The helper does not issue a new physical
acknowledgement. This preserves existing allocation shape while allowing the
new exact-token settlement to occur after actual retirement and before report.

The fixed business control also names the original return/reentry constructor
frontier. The same live Work can return to its original Read, refuse export or
cleanup there without mutation, and resume only in its original business scope.
The original coordinator separately owns the Read and outer transition products;
this does not permit a new execution, token, claim or Source acquisition.
