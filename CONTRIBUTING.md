# Contributing to ActQL

Thank you for your interest. During v0.1 the maintainers are concentrating on
stabilising the Core, and contributions work as follows.

- **Issues are open.** Bug reports, passages of the specification that were
  hard to read, and facts about a Connector's system are the most useful
  contributions right now. Anything that would change language meaning, a
  wire format or a contract starts as an issue, where the compatibility
  impact is agreed.
- **Pull requests are not accepted during v0.1.** After v0.1 the
  maintainers may take in a small number of changes,
  but the Core (language meaning, contracts, the Hosts) stays
  maintainer-driven by design.
- **The ecosystem is where contributions are welcome broadly:** Connectors,
  SDKs, templates and tools around ActQL.

The working reference for changes in this repository, including its layout,
build, tests and house rules, is [docs/contributing/README.md](docs/contributing/README.md).
