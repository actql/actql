# Local Relation physical storage

Local Relation execution retains immutable row tables as its default storage.
Private numeric-column completion adapters can preserve typed producer output;
the broader Value-column and numeric/boolean layouts remain experiments, not
language types or public backend-selection options. The
[completion boundary](execution-completion-storage.md) separates success
ownership from physical representation.

The checked pipeline, owned ordering evidence, shared AoA fragment and logical
resource contracts determine meaning. A physical representation must not alter
row-major binding evaluation, the first evaluated failure, lazy branches, duplicate
occurrences, stable ties, Parameter/producer ownership or canonical identity.
Small result selection does not waive earlier evaluation or input validation.

The private indexed-row and sequence boundaries permit a representation to supply
cell access, construction and release without implementing another checker,
comparison law, take-completion strategy or operation scheduler. Completed output
publication remains atomic and outside experimental storage callbacks.

Shared buffers require physical lifetime accounting distinct from logical table
occurrences. Conversion must include source and destination overlap; a composed
selection must retain and charge the backing storage until its final owner is gone.
Reusing admitted input does not reuse a spent invocation budget.

A successful output may outlive its provider's temporary workspace. Once the
original Model capture is persisted, its publication aliases and Model borrow
can retire while the independent Content owner and journal continuation remain.
The same Local execution can then advance bounded scratch retirement and reconcile
only witnessed temporary execution storage. This preserves the completed output,
its original holding and all consumed work and transfer. The scratch refresh
returns no output-close or physical-release acknowledgement; a pending provider,
reader or nonprogressing continuation keeps its original reservation.

## Funded row publication and exact sealed reload

Physical batching admits the same measured full row before its first payload,
then reuses that row plan while draining bounded fragments. A row may span the
existing physical frames; concatenation, canonical hash and occurrence order
remain unchanged. Aggregate byte, node and depth checks still apply. A frame
that cannot hold one actual fragment refuses instead of yielding indefinitely.
Old and new chunks coexist under the original publication reservation, and
cancellation or current refusal preserves that original row and cleanup custody.

Reload constructs its framer only after the original sealed-content grant has
succeeded. The buffer bound is the smaller of that exact sealed byte geometry
and every explicit original byte/row limit. It cannot inflate a small policy or
construct a buffer after refusal. This changes neither Store capacity nor work
policy, the physical frame guard, Source custody or Compute execution. No Source
or Model replay supplies the publication.

After every append and the same Store seal have acknowledged the exact body,
the existing source-release transition consumes the original export receipt.
Its drained builder, taken output and completed export copies no longer coexist
with receiving construction. The same Task reservation keeps that backing and
reuses only this completed export delta for reload. Earlier transport and import
prefixes remain coheld; the larger of the retired delta and new receiving demand
stays charged. Saved DATA, a pending append, failed seal, cancellation or a refused
current fence cannot select this transition. No physical release, new grant or
future-frame allocation is inferred. These are logical backing quantities under
the existing tariffs, rather than process heap measurements. Named batching and
reload regressions are Source references; runtime and normal artifact acceptance
are separate.

The storage decision is conservative: lower construction counts alone are not a
sufficient reason to replace rows. The bounded workloads use shared row-index
algorithms already, and admission, row-major expression execution and conversion
can dominate the enclosing cost. A future representation change needs evidence of
an end-to-end benefit without semantic or cross-target regressions, rather than
an isolated transposition or allocation result. It must not create permanent dual
product engines merely to improve a selected benchmark.

Measurements and verification records belong in
[the integrated report](../../benchmarks/local-relation/REPORT.md), separate
from these architectural boundaries.
